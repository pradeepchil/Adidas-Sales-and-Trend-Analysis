**Adidas Sales & Profit Analysis**
**Project Overview**

This project analyzes Adidas sales and profit data across products, regions, retailers, and sales channels to identify key performance drivers, seasonal trends, and actionable growth opportunities.

**Objectives**

Understand overall sales, profit, and units sold performance

Identify top and underperforming products

Analyze regional and retailer-wise contribution

Evaluate sales trends by month and sales channel

Provide clear, data-driven business recommendations

**Tools Used**

SQL – data extraction and aggregation

Power BI – data modeling, visualization, and dashboarding

**Key Insights**

Men’s Street Footwear is the top-performing product category

West region contributes the highest share of sales (~30%)

Online sales channel performs the best (~39%)

Sales peak during specific months (May, July, December) with noticeable dips from July to October

**Action Recommendations**

Run targeted promotions during low-performing months

Focus campaigns on underperforming but high-potential categories

Strengthen online channel offerings and refresh collections

Apply product and city-level promotions in high-performing regions
